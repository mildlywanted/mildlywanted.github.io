Mildly Wanted — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Mildly Wanted ("the app") turns short gameplay highlights into vertical videos and sends them to a TikTok account's inbox as drafts. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data the app receives from TikTok, with your permission

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • Video and profile statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, follower counts) to track how posts perform.
  • A list of your public videos, to match drafts with the posts you published.

How it is used

Only to upload drafts to your inbox and to report on how your videos perform. The app does not sell, rent or share your data, and does not use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are held by the app's connection provider (Composio) so the app can act on your behalf. Performance numbers are kept in the app's own records for reporting. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time, and it is deleted when you disconnect the app.

Revoking access

You can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services).

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13 (or the minimum age TikTok requires in your country).

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.

Contact
