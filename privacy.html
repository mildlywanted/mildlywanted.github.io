Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what data Mildly Wanted ("the app", "we") uses when you connect your TikTok account.

Data we receive from TikTok, with your permission

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account you're posting to.
  • Profile details: username, bio and profile link, shown on your account page.
  • Stats: follower, following, like and video counts, shown on your account page.
  • Your public videos: title, cover image, link and view, like, comment and share counts, shown in "Your recent videos".

Videos you upload

When you press Post or Send to TikTok drafts, your browser uploads the video file directly to TikTok. Video files do not pass through or get stored on our servers.

How your data is stored

We don't keep a database of users. Your TikTok access tokens are stored only in an encrypted, HttpOnly cookie in your own browser, and the profile and stats above are fetched from TikTok each time you open the page and are not saved. We don't sell, rent or share your data and don't use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Press Disconnect to revoke the app's access and delete the cookie, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age TikTok requires where you live.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
